# Android demo checklist

- Use an Android emulator for the initial software demo, or a physical Android device for final hardware validation.
- Keep the phone upright, stable, connected to power, and online.
- Keep front and rear cameras unobstructed.
- Grant microphone, camera, notification, and Wake Lock-related permissions.
- Use separate beneficiary and caregiver test accounts.
- Verify a low-severity alert appears without a response flow.
- Verify a high-severity alert escalates after the two-minute response window.
